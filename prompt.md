You are the lead creative director, senior frontend engineer, 3D web experience designer, motion designer, and performance engineer for this project.

You are building an award-level interactive Diwali website called:

THE LIGHT WITHIN

This is a competition submission.

The goal is NOT to create a normal Diwali landing page.

The goal is to create a cinematic, immersive, interactive digital Diwali experience where the entire environment is built around one central idea:

The diya, flame, gold, rangoli and fireworks are the sources of visual energy.

+

+
deep maroon
+
burnished gold
+
subtle particles
+
cinematic depth
+

The website should look beautiful even when almost everything is dark.

7. HERO DIYA
============================================================

The diya is the most important object in the entire website.

It must look premium.

It should feel like a handcrafted Indian clay/brass diya.

Use Three.js / React Three Fiber.

Preferred material:

dark bronze / warm brass / deep terracotta with subtle metallic response.

The diya should contain:

- rim
- inner oil surface
- wick
- flame
- subtle surface imperfections
- realistic contact with the ground

Use physically believable proportions.

============================================================
8. DIYA LIGHTING
============================================================

The diya must actually feel like the source of light.

Use:

1 primary warm point light

plus:

soft emissive flame

plus:

subtle local glow

plus:

subtle reflected illumination

plus:

contact shadow

Do NOT use 20 dynamic lights.

Use lighting economically.

The closer the user gets to the diya, the more visible its warm illumination should become.
